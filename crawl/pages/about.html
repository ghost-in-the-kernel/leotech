Our story

Law enforcement has been last to adopt technology for decades. While the private sector races ahead with AI innovation, those who protect us have been stuck with outdated tools that leave them years behind.

We started with a simple observation: incarcerated individuals control criminal operations through recorded phone lines. These communications represent the world’s largest concentration of criminally-minded activity—all on recorded lines, all legally accessible.

But access wasn’t enough. Traditional keyword search tools delivered thousands of results that investigators had to manually review. What was needed was true artificial intelligence—AI that understands context, learns slang automatically, and surfaces only the intelligence that matters.

That’s what we built

Today, VerusAI transforms investigation from weeks to seconds. We’re not just making law enforcement more efficient—we’re saving lives, preventing crimes, and giving communities the protection they deserve.

And we’re just getting started_
