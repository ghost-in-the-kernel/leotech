Solving the real challenges facing today's agencies

From contraband investigations to suicide prevention to cross-agency intelligence, VerusAI delivers the actionable insights law enforcement needs to protect communities and save lives.

AI for wellness

AI for wellness helps agencies move from reactive response to proactive care by identifying risk earlier, enabling timely intervention, and giving staff the time and insight needed to improve safety and support recovery.

AI for communications

Turn every conversation into actionable intelligence. Monitor, transcribe, and analyze inmate communications at scale—understanding context, code language, and meaning to surface threats in seconds.

AI for video

See what cameras alone can't tell you. Analyze footage in real time and retrospectively—detecting behavior, movement, and environmental changes to automate operations and reduce manual workload.

AI for facility operations

All your data. One operational view. Consolidate siloed data sources into a single AI-assisted dashboard that surfaces threats, correlates risk across systems, and coordinates response in real time.

AI for voice biometrics

Identify who's speaking—even when they don't want to be identified. Voice biometric analysis for caller verification, identity fraud detection, and investigative voice matching across communications data.
