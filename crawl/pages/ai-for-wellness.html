Protecting life requires earlier visibility and faster action

Correctional leaders are responsible for protecting individuals in their care while operating in environments defined by staffing shortages, rising mental health needs, and increasing oversight. Traditional approaches rely on manual observation, delayed reporting, and reactive intervention, which limits the ability to identify risk before it becomes a crisis.

LeoTech provides a different approach. By continuously monitoring communications and behavior across the facility, agencies gain real time visibility into early indicators of mental health distress, suicide risk, and self-harm. This enables staff to act sooner and with greater confidence.

AI for wellness solution use cases

Suicide risk detection and early intervention

Identify language patterns, behavioral changes, and environmental signals that may indicate suicide risk. Surface these indicators early so staff can intervene before a crisis develops and take appropriate action to protect individuals in their care.

Self harm detection across communications and video

Detect signals of self-harm through both what is said in communications and what is observed through video. Combine these insights to provide a more complete understanding of risk and enable faster, more informed intervention.

Behavioral change and distress monitoring

Track shifts in communication patterns, tone, and activity to identify individuals experiencing mental health distress. Recognize early warning signs such as withdrawal, isolation, or escalating behavior and surface them for staff awareness.

High risk individual prioritization

Continuously analyze data to identify and prioritize individuals who may require immediate attention. Ensure staff focus on the highest risk situations without being overwhelmed by unnecessary alerts or noise.

Housing unit and facility level risk visibility

Identify patterns of behavior and activity across housing units, facilities, and statewide systems. Detect spikes in distress, conflict, or instability to support proactive staffing decisions and targeted intervention strategies.

Post incident review and prevention insights

Analyze communications and video leading up to incidents to understand what signals were present and how situations developed. Use these insights to improve future response, training, and prevention strategies.

Support for mental health and wellness programs

Provide staff and leadership with insights that support mental health initiatives, wellness checks, and rehabilitation programs. Enable more informed engagement with individuals based on observed behavior and communication patterns.
