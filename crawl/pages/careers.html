At LeoTech, we are passionate about building technology solutions that solve real-world problems in the public safety sector. Help us build safer communities and the future of public safety.

Get to know us

LeoTech is leading the effort to assist public safety efforts around the nation. We bring industry expertise and innovative technology to address real-world problems public safety agencies face every day.

Our search and analytics platform, Verus, has been used to assist in homeland security, provide assistance for mental health, and increase safety and awareness of public safety.

Everything we do and build is aligned with our mission and the goals of our public safety partners. We work hand-in-hand to build technology solutions that drive the future of public safety.

What’s in it for you

Modern tech stack

We are an AWS enterprise partner and host multiple tech stacks tailored to
our teams. Technologies we use: Elixir, ReactJS, Serverless, Kafka, DynamoDB, PostgreSQL, ElasticSearch, HuggingFace, and EKS, to name a few.

Unique datasets

We are a data mining company at heart, and we work with some of the most unique datasets you’ll see. You’ll enjoy the challenge of working with and deriving value from public safety-focused datasets.

Real-world results

You’ll be building technical solutions for real-world problems while collaborating 
in a team environment with diverse professionals of all technical backgrounds.

Impact

You’ll have a direct role in positively impacting the public safety sector by applying technology to help society and keep our communities safe.

Flexibility

We have a hybrid work culture plus an amenity-rich office with tons of extra perks, perfect for team-building and collaboration.
