Product & Services Overview

LEO Technologies Verus Software Subscription Service

VERUS is a Search and Analytics Platform. Verus is a proprietary, patented technology and process, which includes software and services that provide law enforcement entities with:

  • Automated transcription of all inmate authorized communications originating within a correctional facility.
  • Software that is able to search authorized inmate communications.
  • Model driven analytical processing that provides notifications in near real time about past or potential criminal events, mental health crises, officer/inmate/facility security, PREA, and more.
  • The ability to continuously monitor inmate communications 24 hours a day, 365 days a year by using keywords or phrases.
  • Artificial intelligence and machine learning capabilities.

Related Services

The following related services will be provided at no additional cost to a Customer purchasing the VERUS Software subscription under this Contract. LeoTech shall:

  • Implement and interface the VERUS Software with certain inmate call data collected by the Designated Facility’s phone provider as identified by Customer.
  • Provide adequate personnel and services to train Designated Facility Staff in the use of the VERUS Software.
  • Provide adequate personnel and services to support Customer’s use of the VERUS Software.
  • Produce certified reports depicting its reporting and notifications on a monthly, quarterly, and yearly basis.
